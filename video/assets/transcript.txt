Dussehre pe poore India mein Ravan jalta hai.
Par kuch jagah uski aarti hoti hai!
Dussehra matlab burai par achhai ki jeet.
Toh koi Ravan ko pooj kaise sakta hai?
Noida ke Bisrakh ko Ravan ka janmasthan maana jaata hai.
Wahan Ravan ghar ka beta hai.
Mandsaur ko Mandodari ka maayka maana jaata hai.
Matlab Ravan wahan ka damaad!
Aur damaad ko koi jalata hai kya?
Kanpur mein ek mandir saal mein sirf ek din khulta hai...
...Dussehre ke din.
Wahan Ravan Shiv bhakt ki tarah pooja jaata hai.
Baijnath mein maana jaata hai, Ravan ne yahin Shiv ki tapasya ki.
Aur Gadchiroli ke Gond aadivasi Ravan ko apna purvaj maante hain.
Toh Ravan villain hai ya hero, yeh depend karta hai aap kahan khade ho.
