## Three candidate structures
1. **An overworld tour**: a top-down Lucknow map, each fact a town entered by a door. Pretty, but the film is about *depth*, and a top-down map flattens it.
2. **A turn-based duel**: hero vs "JAGAH KI KAMI". Fun for 10 s, then the record and the cost don't fit a battle.
3. **A dungeon descent (chosen)**: the station is a dungeon, B1–B5, and the film keeps going *down*: the tilt from the street to −122 ft, the side view of stacked tracks, the record screen comparing two dungeons' depths, the cross-section of foundations and pipes the tunnel must pass under, and the long stairs back up to daylight. The vertical frame is a shaft.

## Logline and arc
A city with no room left to spread sideways builds its metro straight down — deeper than any in India.
- Hook (0–7): street level → integer-pixel tilt down past a 12-storey building to a depth meter reading −122 FT.
- Why (7–17): encounter swirl → enemy "JAGAH KI KAMI"; top-down Pandeyganj: two trains can't fit side by side (error buzz).
- How (17–27): side view, two tracks stacked, trains crossing; stat screen: 5 floors, ₹400 crore ticking up.
- Record (27–38): two "save files": HOWRAH 33 m under the river vs LUCKNOW 37 m → NEW RECORD level-up fanfare.
- General reason (38–47): cross-section, foundations and pipes above, the tunnel boring under them.
- Ending (47–56): the long stairs; then a menu: SUBSCRIBE ▶ Whysplained, cursor selects, save chime.
