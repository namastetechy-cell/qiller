Pehle, rule.
1 October 2026 se FEMA ke naye regulations lagu hue.
Isme RBI ne services export ko bhi EDF framework mein le liya.
EDF matlab Export Declaration Form.
Pehle yeh zyada tar goods export walon ke liye tha.
Ab idea yeh hai ki agar aap India se videshi
client ko service dete ho aur paisa bahar se aata hai,
toh woh service export ho sakta hai,
aur uski jaankari bank ke through deni pad sakti hai.

RBI ne aisi baat boli jisne YouTubers ke liye picture badal di.

Doosra: foreign brand deals.
Agar koi videshi brand seedha aapko sponsored video ke paise bhejta hai,
toh kai experts ise service export maante hain.
Yahan dhyan dena zaroori hai.
Teesra: Indian brand deals.
Agar brand India ka hai aur paisa rupay mein India se aata hai,
toh yeh export hi nahi hai.
Is rule se iska lena dena nahi.
Aur ek baat.
Agar aapne apna channel company, LLP ya firm ke naam pe chalaya hai,
toh RBI ke statement ke hisaab se businesses abhi bhi cover ho sakte hain.
GST wale sole proprietors ka final jawab RBI ke FAQs mein aayega,
jo abhi aane baaki hain.
Toh YouTubers ko abhi kya karna chahiye?
Chaar steps.
Ek: panic mat karo.
Individual creators ke liye RBI ka statement raahat wala hai.
Do: records rakho.
AdSense payment statements, brand deal invoices, aur bank ke foreign inward remittance documents,
sab sambhal ke.
Teen: apne bank se poocho.
EDF bank ke through jaata hai,
toh bank hi batayega ki aapke AdSense ya brand payment ke liye kya chahiye.
Chaar: channel company ya registered business ke naam pe hai,
ya badi foreign brand deals karte ho, toh CA se zaroor baat karo.
Summary: individual YouTubers ke liye tension kam hai,
foreign brand deals pe dhyan do,
aur AdSense ke liye RBI ke FAQs ka wait karo.
