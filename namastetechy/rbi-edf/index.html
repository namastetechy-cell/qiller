Aap YouTuber ho aur dollar mein kamaate ho?
RBI ke naye EDF rule se neend udne
se pehle,
suniye RBI ne 7 October ko kya kaha.
Kya aapki AdSense earning pe asar padega?
Aaj main aapko bataunga:
yeh rule hai kya,
YouTubers itna dare kyun,
RBI ne baad mein kya clear kiya,
aur aapki har tarah ki YouTube income ka
kya scene hai.
Pehle, rule.
1 October 2026 se FEMA ke naye regulations
lagu hue.
Isme RBI ne services export ko bhi EDF
framework mein le liya.
EDF matlab Export Declaration Form.
Pehle yeh zyada tar goods export walon ke
liye tha.
Ab idea yeh hai ki agar aap India
se videshi
client ko service dete ho aur paisa bahar
se aata hai,
toh woh service export ho sakta hai,
aur uski jaankari bank ke through deni pad
sakti hai.
Ab socho ek YouTuber ki life.
Har mahine Google se AdSense ka paisa aata
hai.
Kabhi koi foreign brand sponsorship ke dollar bhejta
hai.
Toh YouTubers ne socha:
kya ab har payment ke liye bank mein
form bharna padega?
Kuch reports ke hisaab se koi minimum amount
ki chhoot nahi thi.
Chhote creators, jinke paas na CA hai na
accountant, sabse zyada dare.
Ek business news report ne experts ka reaction
likha:
"Fill a form for just being alive."
Par ruko.
Sirf 6 din baad,
7 October ko,
RBI ne aisi baat boli jisne YouTubers ke
liye picture badal di.
Aur isme ek twist hai,
jo aapki income ke type pe depend karta
hai.
Business Today ki report ke mutabik,
RBI ne kaha ki personal nature ke contracts
ke liye individuals is reporting mein shaamil nahi
hain.
Governor ne example diya:
tuition padhana ya chhota software kaam.
Matlab, agar aap ek individual creator ho,
toh abhi ke statement ke hisaab se aap
par yeh EDF wali tension kaafi kam hai.
Ab twist.
Aapki YouTube income ko teen hisson mein samjho.
Pehla: AdSense aur YouTube se aane wala paisa,
jaise ads,
memberships ya Super Chat.
Yeh sabse bada sawaal hai.
Kya platform ka ad revenue service export hai?
Experts ki raay alag hai,
aur RBI ne ad revenue pe abhi tak
alag se kuch clear nahi kiya.
Doosra: foreign brand deals.
Agar koi videshi brand seedha aapko sponsored video
ke paise bhejta hai,
toh kai experts ise service export maante hain.
Yahan dhyan dena zaroori hai.
Teesra: Indian brand deals.
Agar brand India ka hai aur paisa rupay
mein India se aata hai,
toh yeh export hi nahi hai.
Is rule se iska lena dena nahi.
Aur ek baat.
Agar aapne apna channel company,
LLP ya firm ke naam pe chalaya hai,
toh RBI ke statement ke hisaab se businesses
abhi bhi cover ho sakte hain.
GST wale sole proprietors ka final jawab RBI
ke FAQs mein aayega,
jo abhi aane baaki hain.
Toh YouTubers ko abhi kya karna chahiye?
Chaar steps.
Ek: panic mat karo.
Individual creators ke liye RBI ka statement raahat
wala hai.
Do: records rakho.
AdSense payment statements, brand deal invoices,
aur bank ke foreign inward remittance documents,
sab sambhal ke.
Teen: apne bank se poocho.
EDF bank ke through jaata hai,
toh bank hi batayega ki aapke AdSense ya
brand payment ke liye kya chahiye.
Chaar: channel company ya registered business ke naam
pe hai,
ya badi foreign brand deals karte ho,
toh CA se zaroor baat karo.
Summary: individual YouTubers ke liye tension kam hai,
foreign brand deals pe dhyan do,
aur AdSense ke liye RBI ke FAQs ka
wait karo.
Jaise hi FAQs aayenge,
main update video laaunga.
Yeh video legal ya tax advice nahi hai.
Apne case ke liye bank ya CA se
confirm karein.
