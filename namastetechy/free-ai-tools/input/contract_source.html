CONFIDENTIAL · CONTRACT REPORT

Master Services Agreement — Northwind Logistics × Brightpath Software

1. Parties and Scope

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into between Northwind Logistics Pvt. Ltd. (the "Client") and Brightpath Software Solutions LLP (the "Vendor"). The Vendor will design, build and maintain a fleet-tracking platform for the Client's operations across 14 cities, including mobile apps for drivers and a web dashboard for dispatch teams.
