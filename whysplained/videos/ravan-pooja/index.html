Dussehre par poore India mein
Ravan ko jalaya jaata hai.
Par kuch jagah unki aarti
bhi hoti hai!
Dussehra matlab burai par
achhai ki jeet.
To Ravan ko koi
kaise pooj sakta hai?
Noida ke Bisrakh ko
Ravan ka janmasthan maana
jaata hai.
Wahan Ravan ghar ghar
ka beta hai.
Mandsaur ko Mandodari ka
maayka maana jaata hai.
Matlab Ravan wahan ka damaad!
Aur damaad ko koi
jalata hai kya?
Aap soch rahe ho
bas ek-do gaon?
Galat. List lambi hai.
Kanpur mein ek mandir
saal mein sirf ek
din khulta hai...
...Dussehre ke din.
Wahan Ravan ko Shiv
bhakt ki tarah pooja
jaata hai.
Baijnath mein maana jaata
hai, Ravan ne yahin
Shiv ji ki tapasya
ki thi.
Par Gadchiroli ke kuch
Gond aadivasi Ravan ko
apna purvaj maante hain.
Ek hi kirdaar,
alag jagah,
alag parampara yahi India
ki khoobsurti hai.
