Wait. Some dentists actually hand out candy on Halloween? Why would they do that?
Because the real problem isn't the candy. It's the timing.
Mouth bacteria eat sugar and make acid, and that acid attacks your teeth for about twenty minutes after you snack.
One big candy session means one acid attack. Grazing all day means dozens.
And chocolate melts away fast. Sticky taffy and sour candy cling for hours.
So many dentists hand out chocolate, not chewy stuff, and say: eat it, then brush.
Others run candy buy-backs, paying kids by the pound and shipping the candy to troops overseas.
